# Redistribution of source material and model weights

The repository contains **no uploaded legal text and no model weights**. The content package is built locally and provisioned separately.

| Material | Basis found | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Federal enactments and consolidations (Justice Laws PDFs) | The [Reproduction of Federal Law Order (SI/97-5)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/FullText.html) lets anyone reproduce them without charge or permission, if due diligence is exercised for accuracy and the copy isn't presented as an official version. | The app shows an "Unofficial reproduction — not an official version" notice on every federal document. |
| Ontario statutes and regulations (e-Laws) | The King's Printer for Ontario lets anyone reproduce statutes and regulations, on condition of accuracy, the acknowledgement "© King's Printer for Ontario, [year]", and a statement that unofficial copies are not official versions ([Ontario copyright information](https://www.ontario.ca/page/copyright-information)). | The app shows the not-official notice and the acknowledgement on every Ontario document. Confirm the exact acknowledgement year format before distribution. |
| Municipal by-laws (Peel Region, Brampton, Mississauga) and reference conversions | Not established. | Not committed. Provision them only to users your organization authorizes, or get permission from each municipality. |
| Court decisions | Not installed. | Federal court decisions fall under the same federal order. Check the terms for any others. |
| Apple on-device model | Provided by iOS; the app ships no weights. | Not applicable. |

The repository and the uploaded documents have not been published, and no repository has been written to. Publishing either requires your explicit authorization.
